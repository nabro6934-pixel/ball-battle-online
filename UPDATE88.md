# v88 — Mode rules and side objectives

- Objective HUD is in a right sidebar, including compact side placement on phones. Pure elimination/mirror modes keep existing health bars without an extra victory scoreboard. Team colors/names and bounty value badges are visible above active, non-stealthed fighters and in training rosters.
- Crown artwork is enlarged from 32 to 68 px. Ground crown art aligns with its pickup location; a carried crown stays over its owner. Owner death immediately clears ownership and places a new crown at a seeded random safe map location, before respawn can reclaim it.
- Every engine start uses reproducible full-circle random directions. Team placement no longer resets those vectors to forward motion. Friendly-team bodies now separate/bounce too, without friendly damage; existing air/underground/control exceptions remain.
- Random Relay automatically reveals random owned fighters at match start, shows a short round result, then a 3-second countdown. The winner retains the same fighter; only the loser is randomly replaced at the next start, avoiding immediate repeats when alternatives exist. First to three wins, in online and training modes. Online rewards occur only once at the final result. Between-round leaving/reconnection and timers are handled.
- Control radius is 172.5 (150 × 1.15). Any character-circle contact, including a tangent, counts toward capture; contested teams pause progress. Bounty target is 20, with +2–7 current bounty badges.
- Changing Maze is removed from mode catalogs, online queues and practice selection. Legacy wall helpers remain only for old stored compatibility/regression data.

Validation: real engine/training/presentation tests executed in the hosted JavaScript runtime, including retained winner/replaced loser, 3–0/3–2 finishes, random vectors/determinism, teammate collisions, grazing capture, crown relocation, bounty20, final-only online rewards and between-round leave cleanup. Existing v71/v79/v80 mechanics/render tests also executed. A GitHub Actions job runs the complete Node/DOM/backend regression chain before deployment where available; there is no local executor or pixel browser in this session.
